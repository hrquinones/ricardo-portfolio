## Context

Across two roles — Poder Judicial de Santiago del Estero and Prosegur —
Ricardo worked on migrating legacy systems to maintainable, modern
platforms. At Poder Judicial, that meant a PHP 5 codebase with no design
patterns; at Prosegur, it meant migrating legacy systems to corporate
platforms across several LATAM countries.

## Problem

The Poder Judicial codebase's lack of design patterns made maintenance and
new-feature delivery slow and error-prone. At Prosegur, legacy,
country-specific systems limited how consistently the organization could
scale and support its enterprise solutions.

## Constraints

Both migrations had to preserve existing functionality for live systems
already in use — the judicial system's case-management tools and Prosegur's
HR and payroll platform, which served more than 10,000 employees — while the
underlying architecture changed under them.

## Role

Full Stack Developer at Poder Judicial; Senior Developer / Technical Lead at
Prosegur.

## Approach

At Poder Judicial, migrated the PHP 5 codebase to MVC-based frameworks,
applying best practices that made the system easier to maintain and extend.
At Prosegur, led the migration of legacy systems to corporate platforms
across multiple LATAM countries as part of a broader modernization effort.

## Architecture

- Poder Judicial: legacy PHP 5 (no design patterns) migrated to MVC-based
  frameworks.
- Prosegur: legacy, country-specific systems migrated to shared corporate
  platforms, including an HR and payroll system (Meta Peoplenet 7, PL/SQL
  Oracle 11g, JSP, Tomcat) serving over 10,000 employees.

## Key technical decisions

- Prioritized introducing MVC structure over a full rewrite at Poder
  Judicial, so the migration could proceed incrementally on a live system.
- Standardized on shared corporate platforms at Prosegur rather than
  maintaining separate systems per country, trading local customization for
  long-term scalability and support.

## Result

Both systems moved from legacy, hard-to-maintain architectures to
structured, maintainable platforms — the judicial system onto MVC
frameworks, and Prosegur's HR and payroll system onto a shared corporate
platform supporting more than 10,000 employees across LATAM.

## Lessons learned

Modernization doesn't require a rewrite. In both cases, introducing
structure — design patterns in one, a shared platform in the other — was
enough to unlock maintainability without the risk of replacing a live
system outright.
